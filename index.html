BEFORE YOU CALL

A few useful answers.

Routine appointments Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm. This concept does not provide emergency dispatch.

What should I share about my project?

Tell us the age of the home, the affected fixtures or outlets and when you noticed the issue. For a project, a rough room plan helps.

Do you handle small jobs?

Yes. A single fixture, fan or outlet is a welcome place to start. Several small tasks can often be grouped into one visit.

What if I notice sparking or a burning smell?

Keep away from the equipment. For immediate danger, leave the area and contact emergency services. Do not use this demo form for urgent help.
